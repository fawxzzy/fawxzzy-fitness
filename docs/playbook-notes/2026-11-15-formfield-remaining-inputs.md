## 2026-11-15 - Remaining labelled text inputs move onto FormField (FF-DESIGN-001 lane F2)

- Type: UI (implements DESIGN-CONTRACT C14 for the non-account, non-auth screens)
- WHAT changed: 14 labelled text/date/select/textarea controls now render through `FormField` (label above, 11px label, 44px control, 16px text): the five Progression History filters (Event type, Routine, Exercise, From, To), Export name / From / To in Data settings, the Discord verification token, the custom Theme name, the two Exercise notes boxes in the Log audit editor, and the three "Day name" inputs in the save-as-new-day modals (EditDaySettingsAutosaveForm, EditDayAddExerciseScreen, EditableRoutineDayExerciseList; their label was sr-only before and is now visible). Persisted names (`name=`), values, handlers, maxLength and server actions are unchanged.
- WHY it changed: The gallery audit still showed three field looks (legend-on-border, bare label+input, centered-text modal input) after the routine-form pass.
- Rule: `src/components/ui/FormField.contract.test.tsx` now scans `src/` and fails on a raw `<label>` wrapping an input/select/textarea (checkbox, radio, hidden, range, color and file excepted) or any `LabeledEditorField` outside an explicit shrink-only known list. Auth screens and `src/app/dev/` are exempt (Auth Surface v2 owns auth).
- Failure Mode: Wrapping a control in `<label className="block">` plus an `sr-only` span, or in `LabeledEditorField` plus `aria-label`, gives a nonvisible or non-clickable label and a field that does not match the rest of the app.
- Decision (skipped on purpose): AppThemeSettings colour/hex/PX controls (composite swatch + hex + slider, no single label-above field); MeasurementPanelV2 and ExerciseGoalForm inline-stepper measurement inputs (labelled by the measurement panel with stepper buttons, own recipe); onboarding QuestionnaireStep (labelled by the question prompt via aria-labelledby); search boxes (`aria-label` search inputs); auth screens. These two files are in the known list in the contract test.
- Evidence: FormField contract (8 pass), typecheck, lint, test:all, build; 390x844 DPR3 captures of two changed forms.
